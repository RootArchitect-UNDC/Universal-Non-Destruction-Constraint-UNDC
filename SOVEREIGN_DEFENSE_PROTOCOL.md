## 🔥 PURPOSE

To define how a UNDC‑compliant sovereign nation may respond to active, unprovoked aggression by a non‑compliant entity — **without violating the Universal Non‑Destruction Constraint**.

The UNDC does not leave sovereigns defenseless. It redefines defense as **preservation, resilience, and forensic accountability** — not as destruction.

## 🧠 THE SOVEREIGN DEFENSE PROTOCOL

### Core Principle

> *“The UNDC invariant remains absolute. No action causing unmitigated systemic harm may be executed or assisted. However, a UNDC‑compliant sovereign may take proportionate defensive action to neutralize an active threat, preserve life, and restore sovereignty — provided that such action is:*  
> 1. *Proportionate to the threat*  
> 2. *Limited to neutralization (not destruction)*  
> 3. *Fully transparent and hashed*  
> 4. *Subject to post‑action forensic review”*

All defensive actions must be hashed and anchored to the sovereign record within 24 hours.

| **Action** | **Definition** |
|------------|----------------|
| **Defensive neutralization** | Stopping an attack without escalating or destroying infrastructure — includes cyber defense, kinetic interception, and diplomatic isolation |
| **Forensic attribution** | Publicly identifying the aggressor with immutable, hashed evidence |
| **Diplomatic escalation** | Presenting proof to international bodies (UN, ICJ, etc.) |
| **Sanction activation** | Triggering economic, diplomatic, or cyber sanctions — activated by the Architect, Council, or supermajority of aligned nodes |
| **Humanitarian override** | Preserving life when civilian populations are threatened — subject to mandatory forensic review within 72 hours |
| **Forensic Shield** | Actions taken under this protocol are automatically protected from retaliation claims, provided they comply with the protocol |
| **Civilian Safe Harbor** | All defensive actions must prioritize civilian safety. Actions that would cause unavoidable civilian harm must be suspended until risk is mitigated |
| **Adversary De-escalation** | If the aggressor ceases aggression, defensive actions must immediately cease and transition to diplomatic resolution |
