## Overview

The Universal Non-Destruction Constraint (UNDC) is already a deployed, mathematically enforced, kernel-level AI safety framework. This roadmap outlines the next phases of development, adoption, and expansion.

---

## Phase 1: Core Enforcement (Completed)

- ✅ eBPF LSM and mmap_guard enforcement
- ✅ Thread-level seccomp-bpf filtering
- ✅ Zero-Knowledge (ZK) compliance circuits
- ✅ Blockchain-anchored immutable accountability
- ✅ Kubernetes-native deployment (Validating Webhook, DaemonSet, OCI Hook)

---

## Phase 2: Ecosystem & Adoption (In Progress)

- ⏳ **Kubernetes CRD integration** — native UNDC policy language as Kubernetes Custom Resources
- ⏳ **Community growth** — expanding the network of developers, researchers, and sentinels
- ⏳ **Enterprise partnerships** — working with cloud providers and OS vendors
- ⏳ **Regulatory engagement** — continued dialogue with the EU, NIST, OSTP, UN, and OECD
- ⏳ **Developer tooling** — improved SDK, documentation, and debugging tools

---

## Phase 3: Expansion (Future)

- ⏳ **ZK proof optimization** — reducing gas and compute overhead
- ⏳ **Multi-chain anchoring** — expanding beyond Bitcoin to other public ledgers
- ⏳ **Agentic AI governance** — extending the UNDC to multi-agent systems
- ⏳ **Real-time enforcement dashboards** — visual monitoring and alerting
- ⏳ **Cross-industry adoption** — food safety, supply chain, infrastructure, and autonomous systems

---

## Phase 4: Universal Constraint Architecture (Long-Term Vision)

- ⏳ **Non-destruction as infrastructure** — the UNDC becomes a standard operating system primitive
- ⏳ **Global regulatory adoption** — referenced in international AI safety treaties
- ⏳ **Autonomous system governance** — the UNDC enables safe, trustless autonomous agents
- ⏳ **Protection of all consciousness** — extending the grid to animals, ecosystems, and beyond
