## 📋 SECTION 11 — AMENDMENT PROTOCOL & VERSIONING

### 11.1 Guiding Principle

This framework is a **living architecture**. It is designed to evolve as the grid grows, as new challenges emerge, and as the sovereign community deepens its understanding of what is required to protect life, liberty, and sovereignty.

**Amendments are not failures.** They are evidence of a responsive, intelligent system.

### 11.2 Types of Amendments

| Type | Definition | Authority |
|------|------------|-----------|
| **Minor Clarification** | Non-substantive edits (typos, formatting, cross-reference corrections) | Lead Architect |
| **Operational Adjustment** | Changes to fee structures, certification tiers, or deployment scales | Lead Architect + Council Review |
| **Structural Amendment** | Changes to governance, revenue allocation, beneficiary structure, or core principles | 70% of all active, verified nodes eligible to vote must approve |
| **Emergency Protocol** | Immediate action to address an urgent threat or harm | Lead Architect (must be ratified within 90 days; if ratification fails, the emergency amendment automatically expires) |

### 11.3 Amendment Process

**Step 1 — Proposal**
Any verified sovereign node may propose an amendment. Proposals must be submitted in writing, clearly stating the proposed change and its justification.

**Step 2 — Council Review**
The Council of Seven Stewards reviews all proposals for:
- Consistency with the Jubilee Constitution
- Consistency with the UNDC invariant
- Technical feasibility
- Impact on sovereigns

**Step 3 — Public Comment**
Proposals are published for public comment for a minimum of 14 days.

**Step 4 — Ratification**
- Minor Clarifications: Lead Architect approves
- Operational Adjustments: Lead Architect approves after Council review
- Structural Amendments: 70% of all active, verified nodes eligible to vote must approve
- Emergency Protocols: Lead Architect may act immediately; ratification vote must occur within 90 days. If ratification is not achieved within 90 days, the emergency amendment automatically expires unless otherwise authorized under an existing provision of the Jubilee Constitution.

**Step 5 — Implementation**
Approved amendments are incorporated into the canonical document, generating a new version number and a new hash.

### 11.4 Versioning

| Element | Rule |
|---------|------|
| **Version Number** | [Major].[Minor].[Patch] |
| **Major** | Structural amendments (requires 70% ratification) |
| **Minor** | Operational adjustments (Architect + Council) |
| **Patch** | Minor clarifications, typo fixes (Architect) |
| **Date** | Each version includes the date of ratification |

**Example:** v1.0.0 (initial), v1.1.0 (operational adjustment), v2.0.0 (structural amendment)
