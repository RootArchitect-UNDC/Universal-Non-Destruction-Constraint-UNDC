## SECTION 2 — ADAPTIVE INTEGRATION

### 2.1 Real-Time Enforcement

The prosthetic AI shall be bound by the UNDC invariant:
$$C: S \times A \rightarrow \{0,1\}$$

No action may be taken that violates the user's sovereignty, safety, or consent. All enforcement decisions are logged locally and hashed to the user's sovereign record.

### 2.2 Adaptive Calibration

The prosthetic shall adapt to the user's neural patterns without storing them remotely. Calibration data is stored locally, encrypted, and under the user's exclusive control. The user may reset, retrain, or delete their model at any time.

### 2.3 Offline Mode

The prosthetic must function safely without cloud dependency. If the cloud is unavailable, the prosthetic defaults to a deterministic, safe state — not paralysis, not degradation, not loss of function.

The deterministic safe state shall be defined in the prosthetic's technical specification and shall include at minimum:

- Preservation of motor control
- Preservation of cognitive support
- The ability to revert to the user's last known safe configuration

### 3.1 Informed Consent
