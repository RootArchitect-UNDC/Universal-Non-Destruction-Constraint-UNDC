Before asking anyone to follow rules, show them **real, working code** that demonstrates how the UNDC stops destructive actions. Developers need something they can download, test, and trust.

## 4. Make it un‑bypassable
A simple add‑on (shim) can be turned off. The UNDC must be **built deep into the core** of the AI – where the thinking happens – so it can’t be bypassed.
