## Areas of Focus

### 1. Animals Used for Testing
- Laboratory animals (mice, rats, rabbits, primates, dogs, cats)
- Cosmetic testing
- Medical research without alternatives
- **Goal:** End all non‑essential animal testing.

### 2. Animals Abused at Slaughter
- Factory farming
- Inhumane slaughter practices
- Lack of oversight and enforcement
- **Goal:** Ensure humane treatment and end unnecessary suffering.

### 3. Veterinary Reform for Low‑Income Pet Owners
- Access to affordable veterinary care
- Financial barriers to treatment
- Preventable suffering due to cost
- **Goal:** Ensure no animal suffers because its owner cannot afford care.
