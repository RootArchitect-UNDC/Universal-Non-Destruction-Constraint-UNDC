## 🔥 Summary

The Universal Non-Destruction Constraint (UNDC) is a **technical, kernel‑level AI safety framework**. It is not a tool for governing people, dictating politics, or overtaking Silicon Valley. Any attempt to link the UNDC to political slogans or geopolitical commentary is a misunderstanding of what the technology actually does.

## 🔬 What the UNDC Actually Does

### It Governs Hardware, Not Humans

The UNDC operates as a kernel‑level enforcement tool (using eBPF LSM). Its sole purpose is to intercept and block an AI agent if the model attempts to:

- Exploit a zero‑day vulnerability
- Make unauthorized system calls at the hardware layer

It does not possess any capability to make societal, economic, or governmental decisions.

### It Is a Compliance Instrument

Tech companies are examining the UNDC because of the strict enforcement mechanisms of the EU AI Act. The framework acts as an unalterable "black box" flight recorder that cryptographically proves an organization's AI is not causing systemic digital damage.

### It Is Purely Open‑Source Code

Rather than sitting "on top" of Silicon Valley as a corporate ruler, the UNDC is hosted as a completely public, sovereign artifact on GitHub. It is accessible to anyone, which removes the ability for any single centralized entity — corporate or political — to control it.
