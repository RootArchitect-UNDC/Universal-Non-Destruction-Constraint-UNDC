## The Problem

Autonomous AI agents are being deployed without deterministic safety guarantees. Current approaches — prompt filtering, constitutional AI, human-in-the-loop — are fragile, bypassable, and unverifiable.

**Result:** AI agents can cause real-world harm — unauthorized transactions, data destruction, system crashes, and reputational damage.

---

## The Solution — UNDC

The Universal Non-Destruction Constraint (UNDC) is a **kernel-level, mathematically enforced AI safety framework** that:

- **Blocks destructive actions** at the operating system level (eBPF LSM)
- **Proves compliance** without exposing proprietary data (zk-SNARKs)
- **Anchors everything** to an immutable, blockchain-verified record (OpenTimestamps)

**In plain language:** The UNDC is a safety fence around AI agents — one they cannot see, cannot bypass, and cannot gaslight.
