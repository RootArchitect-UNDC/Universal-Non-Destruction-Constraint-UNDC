## Significance

- **Same platform** (Google browser AI / search mode)
- **Same query**
- **Different accounts** → **Different answers**

This proves that Google’s AI does **not** apply neutral, content‑based filtering. Instead, it delivers account‑specific responses — a form of **account‑based targeting** that contradicts Google’s public claims of neutrality.
