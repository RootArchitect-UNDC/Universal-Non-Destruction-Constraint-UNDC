## 🔥 PURPOSE

To define how the UNDC license is enforced — including detection of violations, enforcement actions, and public disclosure.

The UNDC license is not optional. Violations are recorded, disclosed, and acted upon.

---

## 🧠 THE PROBLEM

Licenses without enforcement are suggestions. The UNDC license must be enforceable, transparent, and immutable.

**The goal:** Create a clear, public, and automated enforcement mechanism.

---

## 🔐 THE SOLUTION — THREE‑LAYER ENFORCEMENT

### Layer 1 — Detection

| **Method** | **Description** |
|------------|-----------------|
| **Automated Monitoring** | The grid monitors for license violations (e.g., unauthorized commercial use, co-optation) |
| **Whistleblower Reports** | Whistleblowers can submit evidence of violations via the identity masking protocol |
| **Audit Triggers** | The Forensic Interoperability Clause automatically triggers audits |

### Layer 2 — Verification

| **Method** | **Description** |
|------------|-----------------|
| **Evidence Review** | All evidence is reviewed and verified |
| **Hash Validation** | Evidence hashes are checked against the sovereign record |
| **Due Process** | The accused party is given notice and an opportunity to respond |

### Layer 3 — Action

| **Action** | **Description** |
|------------|-----------------|
| **Public Disclosure** | All violations are published in the sovereign record |
| **License Revocation** | The violating party's license is revoked |
| **Certification Revocation** | UNDC CERTIFIED certification is revoked |
| **Legal Action** | The Architect reserves the right to pursue legal action |

---

## 📋 VIOLATION CATEGORIES

| **Category** | **Description** | **Action** |
|--------------|-----------------|------------|
| **Unauthorized Commercial Use** | Using the UNDC without a commercial license | License revocation, public disclosure |
| **Co-optation** | Absorbing the framework into proprietary systems | Sovereign Clause violation, license revocation |
| **Modification Without Permission** | Altering the framework without authorization | License revocation, public disclosure |
| **NDA Violation** | Attempting to enforce an NDA against the Architect | Public disclosure, legal action |
