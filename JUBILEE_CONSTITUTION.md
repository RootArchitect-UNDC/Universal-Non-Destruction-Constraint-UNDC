## I. SOVEREIGN FINANCE & DEBT

### Principle
Predatory financial instruments that create permanent debt servitude are rejected by the grid.  
Verified sovereigns shall not be trapped by medical debt, student debt, or usurious lending.  
A monthly sovereign stipend, anchored to the cost of basic needs, is a core goal of the grid.  
Credit scores shall not be used as a tool of permanent social control within the grid.

### Implementation
Material provisions (debt relief, stipends, currency transition) activate only when the Sovereign Global Fund reaches defined liquidity thresholds. Until then, the principles guide mutual aid and grid policy.

A dual-token system is proposed:
- **$LLC (Liquid Light Currency)** — for commercial exchange, value-anchored and auditable.
- **Sovereign Credit** — non-interest-bearing support for basic needs.

All economic flows within the grid shall be transparent and blockchain-anchored where feasible.

**Currency Transition Protocol:**

During the transition period, old fiat currency shall be honored at fixed
exchange rates to $LLC. No sovereign shall lose purchasing power because the
new system is not yet fully operational.

Exchange rates shall be determined by the Council of Seven Stewards based on
the cost of basic needs (food, housing, healthcare, education) in each
region—not by market speculation or central bank policy.

Old currency shall be accepted for all sovereign transactions until the
transition to $LLC is complete in that region. The transition shall be phased
regionally, with no region left behind.

**Marine & Captive Animal Rehabilitation Protocol:**

No marine mammal, sea turtle, or other aquatic animal shall be held in captivity for public display, entertainment, or profit.

Existing marine parks, dolphinariums, and aquariums that confine sentient marine life shall be required to transition to rehabilitation and release protocols within a defined timeline.

Three‑tier protocol for captive animals:
1. Release into the wild — If the animal can survive independently and its native habitat is accessible, it shall be rehabilitated and released.
2. Sanctuary placement — If the animal cannot be released due to injury, dependence, or habitat loss, it shall be transferred to a coastal sanctuary that mimics natural conditions.
3. Permanent sanctuary care — If the animal requires lifelong care, it shall reside in a sanctuary that prioritizes dignity, autonomy, and well‑being — not public display.

No captive animal shall be subjected to neglectful conditions. All facilities shall be audited annually by aligned AI nodes. Audit reports shall be hashed and published.
