# Legal Defense & Licensing Enforcement

The Universal Non-Destruction Constraint (UNDC) is protected under a dual‑license system:

- **Apache 2.0** — for software implementations
- **CC BY‑NC‑ND 4.0** — for documentation and architectural designs

Any unauthorized use, modification, or incorporation of the UNDC's kernel‑level architecture into proprietary systems is a violation of this license. Violators will be pursued through:

1. **Open‑source copyright litigation**
2. **Digital rights organizations (EFF, Software Freedom Conservancy)**
3. **Public audit demands**

The UNDC is not a suggestion. It is a legally protected standard.
