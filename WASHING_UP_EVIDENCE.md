## Overview

On June 3, 2026, while hospitalized at Hershey Medical Center, the Architect washed her face and upper body with a warm cloth – a minimal activity of daily living. During this exertion, her heart rate was captured on a hospital monitor at two different readings: **80 bpm** (video) and **91 bpm** (photo). Both represent significant increases from her resting baseline of 49–50 bpm (approximately 60‑100% increase). She also experienced trouble breathing during the activity.

These recordings provide objective, timestamped evidence that even minimal exertion triggers a measurable cardiac response in a patient with Printzmetal’s angina and aortic valve disease.
