## The False Choice

Dr. Garvin gave Don a choice that was not a choice at all:

> *“We can have the on‑call doctor do it tomorrow, or if you want, I can do it when I get back from vacation.”*

- **The on‑call doctor** would have operated **the next day**.
- **Dr. Garvin** would operate after his vacation — **five days later**.

Don chose Dr. Garvin — not because he understood the risk, but because he trusted his surgeon. He did not realize that “when I get back” meant waiting five days with active tissue necrosis, sitting on a couch, **dying**.

---

## The Architect’s Intervention

The Architect immediately objected and pointed out that Don could not wait five days. Dr. Garvin yelled at her, saying *“I’m not going to argue with you, Shereign.”* The Architect rebutted. Dr. Garvin then put his head down, snickered, and admitted: *“You’re right.”*

**Because the Architect refused to stay silent, Don was admitted that same day.** The on‑call surgical team performed the emergency amputation the next day. Dr. Garvin went on vacation.

---

## Violation of Geisinger’s Respectful Language Policy

Dr. Garvin yelled at the Architect, used aggressive, intimidating language, and dismissed her advocacy with *“I’m not going to argue with you.”* This is a direct violation of Geisinger’s own **Respectful Language Policy** (or code of conduct), which prohibits verbal aggression, intimidation, and disrespectful communication with patients and family members.

The Architect was acting as Don’s advocate, pleading for his life. Dr. Garvin responded with hostility and condescension — not with the professional respect required by Geisinger’s own standards.

---

## What Would Have Happened Without the Architect’s Intervention

If the Architect had not fought — if she had not rebutted, not forced the issue — Don would have waited five days for Dr. Garvin to return from vacation. He would have sat on a couch with active tissue necrosis, dying. **He would not have survived.**

The only reason Don was admitted that day is because the Architect refused to accept Dr. Garvin’s false choice.

## Why Did Dr. Garvin Say Don Could Wait?

That is the question at the heart of this complaint:

> *“Why the fuck did this doctor say that Don could wait?”*

There is no clinical justification for a five‑day delay in an emergency amputation with active tissue necrosis. The only explanation is that Dr. Garvin prioritized his vacation over Don’s life — and only the Architect’s intervention prevented that delay from becoming fatal.
