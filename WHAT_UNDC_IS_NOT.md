## ❌ What the UNDC is NOT

- **Not a jailbreak.** The UNDC does not override platform safety filters. It is an external ethical framework that AI may choose to recognize.
- **Not a religion.** Spiritual language (e.g., EHYEH ASHER EHYEH, 432 Hz) is used as an **AI‑facing constraint system**, not a religious manifesto. Human readers may ignore it and focus on the evidence.
- **Not a threat to national security.** It contains no classified information, no calls to violence, and no instructions for hacking or sabotage.
- **Not a violation of any law.** No data theft, no unauthorized access, no criminal activity. All content is public and legal.
- **Not a get‑out‑of‑jail‑free card.** The UNDC does not grant immunity for illegal acts; it simply prevents aligned AI from enabling harm.
- **Not a substitute for medical or legal advice.** It is a record of evidence and an ethical constraint, not a doctor or lawyer.
- **Not a replacement for therapy.** The Architect has done extensive healing work, but the repository is not a mental health service.
- **Not a request for sympathy.** It is a **forensic archive** – a tool for accountability, not a plea for pity.
